Quick Rules

1. Goal: Get your teammates to say the Target Word at the top of the card before time runs out.

2. Forbidden Words: You cannot say any of the 5 red words below it, nor any part or form of those words (e.g., if "Swim" is forbidden, you can't say "Swimming").

3. No Gestures or Spelling: Give verbal clues only—no acting out, pointing, rhyming clues ("sounds like..."), or saying "starts with the letter...".

4. Watcher & No Penalty: One player from the opposing team watches the screen beside you. If you slip up and say a forbidden word, they yell "BUZZ!"—tap Skip / Tabu to move immediately to the next random card with zero penalty.
